Ten lots. Faster hammers.

Silent Bid is built as a short private-estate sale: ten handcrafted levels, each one an item, a climbing price, and a narrower margin for a correct bid.

  1. Opening

    Learn the room

    Early levels teach you to match clues to a range and to wait for the price instead of bidding on instinct.

  2. Middle

    Conflicting evidence

    Indicators start to disagree. Misleading details pull the estimate wide, and the useful range gets harder to trust.

  3. Final

    Quicker sales

    The last lots keep the same irreversible bid, then shorten the time you have to read the room and strike.

Stars, not a second chance

★ ★ ★

A clean, well-timed bid is worth up to three stars. There is no revision after you commit: the price either sat inside the displayed range, or it did not. Observation, timing, and judgment decide the lot.
